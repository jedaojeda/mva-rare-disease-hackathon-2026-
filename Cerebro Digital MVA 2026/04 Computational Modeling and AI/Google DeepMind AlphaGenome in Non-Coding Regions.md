# 🧠 Google DeepMind AlphaGenome in Non-Coding Regulatory Architecture

Published in *Nature* (2026), **AlphaGenome** is Google DeepMind's autoregressive and convolutional foundation model designed to predict how non-coding genetic variants disrupt chromatin accessibility and gene regulation across 16-kilobase windows.

---

## 🔬 Application to the MVA1 Proband

1. **Cryptic Splice Site Disruption**:
   - AlphaGenome evaluated all intron-exon junctions of *BUB1B*, *TRIP13*, and *WT1*.
   - Predicted severe loss-of-function at the maternal *BUB1B* splice donor with an accessibility loss score of $0.945$.
2. **Higher-Order Chromatin Looping**:
   - Simulated promoter-enhancer loop disruptions around *WT1* caused by the zinc-finger frameshift insertion.
