## 🔬 Dual Pathogenic Impact

1. **Genesis of Medullary Nephrocalcinosis**:
   - Abolition of the C2H2 zinc-finger domain required for recognizing GC-rich promoter elements of *TRPV5* and *Calbindin-D28k* ([[Pediatric Medullary Nephrocalcinosis and Renal Challenge]]).
2. **Wilms Tumor Predisposition**:
   - Loss of tumor suppressor transcriptional control over metanephric blastemal differentiation.
