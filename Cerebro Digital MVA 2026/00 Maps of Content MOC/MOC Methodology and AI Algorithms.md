# 🤖 MOC: Methodology and AI Algorithms

This Map of Content details the computational pipeline and integration of state-of-the-art biological foundation AI models (2023–2026) utilized to resolve non-coding regulatory impact and 3D macromolecular structures.

---

## 🧠 Integrated Biological Foundation Models

* [[Google DeepMind AlphaGenome in Non-Coding Regions]]: 16 kb autoregressive/convolutional epigenomic scoring and cryptic splice disruption.
* [[Google DeepMind AlphaMissense in Missense Mutations]]: Structural pathogenicity posterior probability calibrated on AlphaFold.
* [[Arc Institute Evo 2 and Delta Log Likelihood]]: 7B-parameter StripedHyena genomic language model evaluating complex structural indels.
* [[FutureHouse AI PaperQA2 and ChemCrow]]: Automated biomedical literature synthesis and chemoinformatic validation.

## 📐 Scoring Algorithms and Simulation Engines

* [[Integrated Pathogenicity Index IPI Formula]]: Multi-modal ensemble formulation combining AlphaMissense, AlphaGenome, and Evo 2.
* [[Molecular Docking AutoDock Vina and MMFF94]]: High-throughput docking protocol, forcefield energy minimization, and Ki estimation.
