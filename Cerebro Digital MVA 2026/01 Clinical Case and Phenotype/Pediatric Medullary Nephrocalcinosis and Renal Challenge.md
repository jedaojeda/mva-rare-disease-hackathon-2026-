# 🫘 Pediatric Medullary Nephrocalcinosis and the Renal Challenge

One of the most clinically critical and unusual features presented by the index proband is **Bilateral Congenital Medullary Nephrocalcinosis**, sonographically confirmed by hyperechogenicity in the pyramids of Malpighi.

---

## 🔍 Molecular Pathophysiology: The WT1 - TRPV5 Axis

This nephrocalcinosis is not drug-induced, but represents a **genetically driven transport defect**:

1. **Impact of the WT1 Insertion**:
   - The proband harbors a zinc-finger frameshift insertion in the Wilms tumor transcription factor: `chr11:32408514 T>TA (c.1180dupA, p.Arg394fs)`.
2. **Disruption of Distal Tubular Calcium Reabsorption**:
   - WT1 directly transactivates promoters for:
     * **TRPV5**: The apical epithelial calcium channel in the distal convoluted tubule (DCT).
     * **Calbindin-D28k**: The cytosolic calcium shuttle transporting $Ca^{2+}$ across tubular cells.
3. **Clinical Consequence**:
   - Loss of WT1 transcriptional activation abolishes transcellular calcium reabsorption in the DCT, causing **obligate renal hypercalciuria** and intratubular calcium phosphate precipitation.
