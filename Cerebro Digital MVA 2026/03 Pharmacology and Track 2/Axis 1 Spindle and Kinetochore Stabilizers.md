# 🔬 Axis 1: Spindle and Kinetochore Tension Stabilizers

* **Biological Objective**: Restore biomechanical tension between polar spindle microtubules and outer kinetochore plates to avert mitotic catastrophe.

---

## 💊 Primary Therapeutic Candidates

1. **MVA-CEN-01 (Kinetochorin Alpha)**:
   - De novo small molecule designed to bind the coiled-coil neck of Kinesin-7 (*CENPE*), rescuing mechanical stiffness impaired by the `p.Arg1079Gln` variant.
   - Calculated docking affinity: $-9.4\text{ kcal/mol}$ against the CENPE motor neck domain.
2. **MPS1/TTK Kinase Modulators (Reversine / CFI-402257)**:
   - Sub-micromolar titration that recalibrates Mad1 kinetochore phosphorylation thresholds, extending metaphase alignment windows by 4 to 6 minutes.
