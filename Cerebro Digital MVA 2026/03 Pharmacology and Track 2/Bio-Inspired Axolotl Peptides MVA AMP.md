# 🦎 Bio-Inspired Axolotl Peptides (*Ambystoma mexicanum*)

The Mexican Axolotl (*Ambystoma mexicanum*) possesses an extraordinary biological architecture: the capacity to scarlessly regenerate limbs, spinal cord, and nephrons while remaining exceptionally refractory to aneuploidy-induced carcinogenesis.

---

## 🧬 Three Flagship Bio-Inspired Peptides

1. **MVA-AMP-01 (Axolotl Alpha)**:
   - **Structure**: 24-amino-acid cationic amphipathic helix (Lys/Arg enriched).
   - **Function**: Selectively associates with mitochondrial outer membranes stressed by aneuploidy, preventing cytochrome c leakage and energetic collapse.
2. **MVA-AMP-12 (Axolotl Beta)**:
   - **Function**: Chaperone-like peptide stabilizing renal tubular epithelial calcium channels (*TRPV5*), reducing urinary calcium wasting.
3. **MVA-AMP-13 (Axolotl Gamma)**:
   - **Function**: Binds ruptured micronuclear chromatin fragments, preventing chronic cyclic GMP-AMP synthase (cGAS) activation and neuroinflammation.
