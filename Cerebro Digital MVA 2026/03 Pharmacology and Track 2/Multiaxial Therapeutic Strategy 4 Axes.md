# 💊 Multiaxial 4-Tier Therapeutic Strategy for MVA1

Because MVA1 is not a simplistic single-target defect, but a multi-systemic catastrophe in chromosome transmission and tissue homeostasis, the program designed by **Dr. Daniel Antonio Ojeda, MD** deploys a **4-Tier Synergistic Multiaxial Strategy**:

1. **[[Axis 1 Spindle and Kinetochore Stabilizers]]**:
   - Biomechanical restoration of kinetochore tension on misaligned chromosomes.
   - Lead candidates: *MVA-CEN-01 (Kinetochorin Alpha)*, ultra-low-dose microtubule stabilizers (epothilone B), and balanced MPS1 inhibition (*CFI-402257*).
2. **[[Axis 2 Proteasomal and Checkpoint Modulators]]**:
   - Prevention of premature mitotic slippage by stabilizing cyclin B1/securin levels.
   - Candidates: Reversible proteasomal inhibition at sub-therapeutic doses (*bortezomib*) and allosteric APC/C inhibitors (*proTAME*).
3. **[[Axis 3 Tubular Nephroprotection TRPV5 and Calbindin]]**:
   - Halting medullary crystal deposition by restoring active distal tubular calcium reabsorption.
   - Candidates: Low-dose thiazides (chlorthalidone) + VDR/Klotho nuclear agonists (*paricalcitol*) + potassium citrate.
4. **[[Axis 4 Oncologic Surveillance and Early Detection]]**:
   - Strict imaging and liquid biopsy schedule to eradicate solid tumors at Stage I.
