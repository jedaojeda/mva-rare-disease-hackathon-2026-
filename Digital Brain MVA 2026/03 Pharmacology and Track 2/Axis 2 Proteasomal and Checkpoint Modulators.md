# 🔬 Axis 2: Proteasomal and Checkpoint Modulators

* **Biological Objective**: Counteract BUBR1 hypofunction by preventing premature destruction of cyclin B1 and securin via selective, temporal modulation of the APC/C-proteasome axis.

---

## 💊 Mechanism and Lead Compounds

1. **Allosteric APC/C Inhibitors (proTAME / Apcin)**:
   - Disrupt Cdc20 binding to substrate destruction boxes (D-boxes), keeping APC/C inactive while lagging chromosomes remain unattached.
2. **Microdose Proteasome Inhibition**:
   - Ultra-low pulsed dosing of *bortezomib* or non-boronate bioisosteres to prevent premature degradation of the Mitotic Checkpoint Complex (MCC).
