# 🫘 Axis 3: Distal Tubular Nephroprotection and TRPV5 Rescue

* **Biological Objective**: Reduce urinary calcium supersaturation and restore active calcium reabsorption across the distal convoluted tubule (DCT), preventing progression of medullary nephrocalcinosis to chronic kidney disease.

---

## 💊 Triple Nephroprotective Regimen

1. **Low-Dose Thiazides (Chlorthalidone / Hydrochlorothiazide)**:
   - **Mechanism**: Inhibits NCCT in the DCT, hyperpolarizing the basolateral membrane and passively driving apical calcium influx via residual TRPV5 channels.
   - **Efficacy**: Reduces urinary calcium excretion by 45–60%.
2. **Urinary Chelation via Potassium Citrate**:
   - **Mechanism**: Citrate forms soluble complexes with ionized calcium, inhibiting nucleation and aggregation of calcium phosphate/oxalate salts.
3. **Selective Vitamin D Receptor (VDR) Agonists**:
   - *Paricalcitol* or *calcitriol* to stimulate basolateral NCX1 and PMCA1b calcium extrusion back into the peritubular circulation.
