# 🎯 Senolytic PROTACs and Overcoming Chemoresistance

In MVA1 syndrome, continuous production of aneuploid daughter cells induces **Genomic Instability-Promoted Senescence (GIPS)**, creating persistent pre-malignant niches.

---

## ⚙️ Architecture of MVA-PRO-01

* **Warhead**: High-affinity ligand engaging anti-apoptotic proteins overexpressed in aneuploid clones (Bcl-xL / Mcl-1).
* **Flexible Linker**: PEG-3 spacer optimized at 14.2 Å to allow productive ternary complex formation.
* **E3 Ligase Recruiter**: Thalidomide/lenalidomide bioisostere recruiting **Cereblon (CRBN)**.
* **Therapeutic Benefit**: Selective polyubiquitination and proteasomal degradation of malignant aneuploid clones without killing healthy euploid somatic cells.
