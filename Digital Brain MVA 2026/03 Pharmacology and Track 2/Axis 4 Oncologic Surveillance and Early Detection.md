# 🛡️ Axis 4: Proactive Oncologic Surveillance and Early Detection

* **Biological Objective**: Because MVA1 carries an estimated $>35\%$ cumulative incidence of pediatric malignancies (predominantly Wilms tumor and embryonal rhabdomyosarcoma), Axis 4 establishes a strict surveillance net to intercept emerging lesions at Stage I.

---

## 🔍 Core Surveillance Pillars

1. **Four-Month Abdominal & Renal Ultrasound**:
   - Every 3–4 months until 8 years of age, conducted by dedicated pediatric imaging specialists.
2. **Circulating Tumor DNA & Aneuploid Clonal Tracking**:
   - Liquid biopsy tracking for emergent somatic *WT1* loss-of-heterozygosity or *DICER1* secondary hits.
3. **Targeted Senolytic Clearing (PROTACs)**:
   - Selective elimination of aneuploid senescent clones before irreversible malignant transformation ([[Senolytic PROTACs and Chemoresistance]]).
