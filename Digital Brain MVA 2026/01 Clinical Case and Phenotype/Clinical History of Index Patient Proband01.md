# 👶 Clinical History of the Index Proband (PROBAND01)

The index patient (`PROBAND01`, sequencing identifier `WGS_EX2312012`) is a pediatric male diagnosed with **Mosaic Variegated Aneuploidy Syndrome (MVA1)** following extensive multidisciplinary evaluation for profound global developmental delay and dysmorphic features.

---

## 📋 Comprehensive Clinical Case Summary

1. **Perinatal and Growth Trajectory**:
   - Born preterm following a pregnancy complicated by severe symmetrical intrauterine growth restriction (IUGR).
   - Birth weight and occipitofrontal circumference (OFC) significantly below the 3rd percentile ($< -3\text{ SD}$), consistent with congenital microcephaly.
2. **Cardiovascular Findings**:
   - Small perimembranous ventricular septal defect (VSD) with mild left-to-right shunt and stable hemodynamics managed conservatively.
3. **Renal and Urological Manifestations**:
   - High-resolution abdominal sonography demonstrated diffuse, bilateral hyperechogenicity throughout the medullary renal pyramids, diagnostic of **Bilateral Congenital Medullary Nephrocalcinosis**.
   - Serum creatinine within age-appropriate reference ranges, but with documented hypercalciuria (elevated urinary calcium-to-creatinine ratio).
4. **Cytogenetic Confirmation and Mosaicism**:
   - G-banded karyotype on peripheral blood lymphocytes revealed $>30\%$ aneuploid metaphases, exhibiting variable, non-clonal monosomies and trisomies (predominantly involving chromosomes 7, 8, 11, and 18).
   - Metaphase spreads demonstrated widespread **Premature Chromatid Separation (PCS)**, the cellular hallmark of *BUB1B* loss-of-function.
