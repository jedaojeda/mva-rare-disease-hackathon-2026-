# 📋 MOC: Clinical Surveillance and Management Protocols

This Map of Content gathers practical, clinician-led management protocols designed for the treating pediatric subspecialists (pediatricians, nephrologists, oncologists, geneticists, and cardiologists) caring for the index patient.

---

## 🏥 Actionable Clinical Protocols

* [[Anderson Carr Renal Surveillance Protocol]]: High-resolution renal ultrasound every 3 months, urine calcium/creatinine ratios, and stone prophylaxis.
* [[Oncologic Screening for Wilms Tumor and Rhabdomyosarcoma]]: Surveillance schedule (abdominal ultrasound every 3 months until age 8, soft tissue exams).
* [[Congenital Heart Disease and Neurodevelopment Support]]: Hemodynamic monitoring of VSD, early developmental therapy, and nutritional optimization.
* [[Genetic Counseling and Family Planning for MVA]]: Autosomal recessive recurrence risk assessment (25%) and IVF/PGD reproductive options.
