# 💊 MOC: De Novo Pharmacology and 3D Molecules (Track 2)

This Map of Content consolidates the translational therapeutic program developed for the child with MVA1, approaching the disease not as an isolated symptom, but as a systemic multiaxial biological deregulation.

---

## 🏛️ The 4-Tier Synergistic Therapeutic Strategy

* [[Multiaxial Therapeutic Strategy 4 Axes]]: High-level conceptual overview.
* [[Axis 1 Spindle and Kinetochore Stabilizers]]: Microtubule dynamics, MPS1/TTK kinase modulation, and tension rescue.
* [[Axis 2 Proteasomal and Checkpoint Modulators]]: Cyclin B1 retention, APC/C allosteric inhibition, and mitotic slippage mitigation.
* [[Axis 3 Tubular Nephroprotection TRPV5 and Calbindin]]: Distal convoluted tubule calcium reabsorption rescue to halt nephrocalcinosis.
* [[Axis 4 Oncologic Surveillance and Early Detection]]: Proactive imaging and biomarker screening for early Wilms/rhabdomyosarcoma detection.
