# 👨‍👩‍👦 Genetic Counseling and Reproductive Family Planning

---

## 🧬 Inheritance Pattern and Recurrence Risks

1. **Autosomal Recessive Architecture**:
   - Both parents are healthy, obligate asymptomatic heterozygous carriers:
     * Mother: Carrier of `chr15:40209701 T>G` ([[Maternal Variant chr15 40209701 T G]]).
     * Father: Carrier of `chr15:40220612 T>G` ([[Paternal Variant chr15 40220612 T G]]).
2. **Recurrence Probability in Subsequent Pregnancies**:
   - **25%** risk of an affected child with MVA1 (inheriting both pathogenic alleles).
   - **50%** probability of healthy carrier offspring.
   - **25%** probability of offspring free of both pathogenic variants.

---

## 🏥 Actionable Reproductive Options

* Preimplantation Genetic Diagnosis (PGD) coupled with in vitro fertilization (IVF) to select embryos negative for the biallelic combination.
* Early prenatal diagnostics via chorionic villus sampling (weeks 11–13) or amniocentesis (week 16).
