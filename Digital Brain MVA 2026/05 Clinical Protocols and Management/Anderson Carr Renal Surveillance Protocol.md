# 🫘 Anderson-Carr Renal Surveillance Protocol

Formulated from an Internal Medicine perspective by Dr. Daniel Antonio Ojeda, this protocol outlines the clinical monitoring strategy required to preserve the proband's glomerular filtration rate:

---

## 📅 Chronological Monitoring Schedule

1. **High-Resolution Renal Ultrasound (Every 3 Months)**:
   - Staging of medullary hyperechogenicity density according to the **Anderson-Carr Scale** (Grades I through IV).
   - Early detection of secondary nephrolithiasis or urinary tract obstruction.
2. **Comprehensive Serum Biochemistry (Every 3 Months)**:
   - Serum creatinine, blood urea nitrogen (BUN), cystatin C, and eGFR calculation via pediatric Schwartz equation.
   - Serum electrolytes: total and ionized calcium, phosphate, magnesium, potassium, and venous blood gas (ruling out distal renal tubular acidosis).
3. **Urinary Excretion Profiling (Every 3 to 6 Months)**:
   - Spot morning urine calcium-to-creatinine ratio (Target: $< 0.20\text{ mg/mg}$).
   - 24-hour urinary citrate, oxalate, and calcium clearance.

---

## 💊 Active Therapeutic Measures
* Optimized oral enteral hydration (1500 to 1800 ml/m²/day).
* Low-dose thiazide therapy ([[Axis 3 Tubular Nephroprotection TRPV5 and Calbindin]]).
* Potassium citrate as an alkalizing urinary chelator.
