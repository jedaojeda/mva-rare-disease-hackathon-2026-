# 🛡️ Oncologic Screening for Wilms Tumor and Rhabdomyosarcoma

Given the marked lifetime risk of pediatric malignancies secondary to checkpoint failure and *WT1* mutation, the following prophylactic screening schedule is mandated:

---

## 🔍 Modality and Surveillance Timetable

1. **Wilms Tumor (Nephroblastoma)**:
   - **Modality**: Dedicated bilateral renal ultrasound every 3 months until 8 years of age.
   - **Alarm Signs**: Well-demarcated hypoechoic intrarenal mass, macro/microscopic hematuria, or sudden systemic hypertension.
2. **Embryonal Rhabdomyosarcoma**:
   - **Modality**: Thorough monthly pediatric physical exam palpating for soft-tissue masses in head, neck, retroperitoneum, and pelvic/genitourinary regions.
   - **Imaging**: Annual or targeted whole-body magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) upon any palpable asymmetry.
3. **Leukemia / Lymphoma Surveillance**:
   - Complete blood count with peripheral smear review every 2 months to monitor for cytopenias or circulating atypical blasts.
