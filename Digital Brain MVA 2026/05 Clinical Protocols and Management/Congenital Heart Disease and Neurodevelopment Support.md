# 👶 Congenital Heart Disease and Neurodevelopmental Support

---

## ❤️ Management of Ventricular Septal Defect (VSD)

* Color Doppler echocardiography every 6 months by pediatric cardiology to monitor defect caliber, shunt velocity, and pulmonary arterial pressures.
* Subacute bacterial endocarditis (SBE) prophylaxis prior to invasive or dental procedures.

---

## 🧠 Neurodevelopmental Optimization

* Continuous physical, occupational, and speech therapy initiated in early infancy.
* Pediatric neurology monitoring and baseline electroencephalogram (EEG) to rule out subclinical seizure activity.
* Hypercaloric nutritional formulas to counterbalance the increased metabolic expenditure of inefficient mitotic cycles.
