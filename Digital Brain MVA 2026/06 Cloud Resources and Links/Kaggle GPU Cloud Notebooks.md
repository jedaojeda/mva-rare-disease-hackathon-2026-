# 🚀 Cloud GPU Compute Notebooks on Kaggle

To guarantee 100% computational auditability, all pipelines were executed on NVIDIA Cloud GPUs on Kaggle:

1. **Track 1 — WGS 85 GB AlphaGenome DeepScan**:
   - URL: [https://www.kaggle.com/code/jedaojeda/mva-wgs-alphagenome-deepscan](https://www.kaggle.com/code/jedaojeda/mva-wgs-alphagenome-deepscan)
   - Streams 8 raw NovaSeq lanes (~560 million reads), base-by-base fastp C++ audit, and AlphaGenome epigenomic scoring.
2. **Track 1 — Variant Calling & IPI Inference Pipeline**:
   - URL: [https://www.kaggle.com/code/jedaojeda/mva-hackathon-2026-pipeline](https://www.kaggle.com/code/jedaojeda/mva-hackathon-2026-pipeline)
   - Consensus variant calling, IPI index calculation, and automated CSV leaderboard generation.
3. **Track 2 — Repurposing & FutureHouse AI Chemoinformatics**:
   - URL: [https://www.kaggle.com/code/jedaojeda/mva-drug-repurposing-futurehouse](https://www.kaggle.com/code/jedaojeda/mva-drug-repurposing-futurehouse)
   - PaperQA2 literature mining, ChemCrow validation, and AutoDock Vina macromolecular docking.
