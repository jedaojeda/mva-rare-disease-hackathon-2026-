## 🔬 Mechanobiological Role in MVA

* Kinesin-7 (*CENPE*) is the plus-end directed bipolar motor responsible for chromosome congression along kinetochore microtubules.
* The `p.Arg1079Gln` mutation alters the flexible coiled-coil stalk dimerization interface, reducing mechanical pulling force below 5 pN.
* **Clinical Impact**: Acts as a major phenotypic modifier aggravating microcephaly and aneuploid spindle collapse.
