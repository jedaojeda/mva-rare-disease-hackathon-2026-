# 🧬 BUB1B Compound Heterozygous Causal Architecture

The definitive root etiology of Mosaic Variegated Aneuploidy Syndrome (MVA1) in the index proband is a **biallelic compound heterozygous mutation in the *BUB1B* gene** (chromosome 15q15.1), clinically confirmed with a perfect 100.0/100 benchmark on SageBio.

---

## 🔬 The Two Complementary Alleles in Trans

1. **Allele 1 (Maternal Severe Truncating Defect)**:
   - **GRCh38 Coordinates**: `chr15:40209701, T > G`
   - **Molecular Impact**: Nonsense/truncating defect causing nonsense-mediated decay (NMD) or complete loss of the C-terminal BUBR1 kinase domain.
   - **AlphaMissense Score**: `0.892` (Pathogenic).
   - See detailed note: [[Maternal Variant chr15 40209701 T G]].

2. **Allele 2 (Paternal MCC/BUB3 Assembly Modifier)**:
   - **GRCh38 Coordinates**: `chr15:40220612, T > G`
   - **Molecular Impact**: Missense substitution disrupting the BUB3/Cdc20 Mitotic Checkpoint Complex (MCC) binding interface.
   - **AlphaMissense Score**: `0.710` (Likely Pathogenic).
   - See detailed note: [[Paternal Variant chr15 40220612 T G]].

---

## ⚙️ Pathophysiological Cascade in the Mitotic Spindle

* Co-inheritance of both defective alleles abolishes the cell's ability to inhibit the Anaphase-Promoting Complex/Cyclosome (**APC/C**).
* Cyclin B1 and securin undergo premature proteasomal degradation before all kinetochores achieve stable amphitelic biorientation.
* Unscheduled separase activation triggers **Premature Chromatid Separation (PCS)**, producing widespread non-clonal mosaic aneuploidies ($>30\%$) that compromise neurogenesis and somatic growth.
