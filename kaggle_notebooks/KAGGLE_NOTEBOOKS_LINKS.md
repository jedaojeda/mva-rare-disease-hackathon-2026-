# Official Cloud Compute & Kaggle Notebooks
**Competition**: Sage Bionetworks MVA Rare Disease Hackathon 2026  
**Principal Investigator**: Daniel Antonio Ojeda, MD (Internal Medicine Specialist, ULA - IAHULA)  

To ensure 100% end-to-end auditability and computational reproducibility, all data processing, foundation AI inference, and molecular docking workflows were executed on Kaggle Cloud GPUs (NVIDIA T4 / P100) and are publicly accessible via the official Kaggle kernels:

---

### 1. Track 1: WGS 85 GB AlphaGenome DeepScan & Flowcell Audit
- **Direct Kaggle URL**: [https://www.kaggle.com/code/jedaojeda/mva-wgs-alphagenome-deepscan](https://www.kaggle.com/code/jedaojeda/mva-wgs-alphagenome-deepscan)
- **Slug**: `jedaojeda/mva-wgs-alphagenome-deepscan`
- **Focus**: Multi-lane streaming audit of 85.0 GB raw FASTQ files (L001-L004 NovaSeq 6000), fastp Q30 quality control (>94.0%), BWA-MEM2 alignment, Sambamba deduplication, and 16 kb Transformer epigenomic scoring via AlphaGenome.

---

### 2. Track 1: Variant Calling, Trimodal AI Scoring & IPI Prioritization
- **Direct Kaggle URL**: [https://www.kaggle.com/code/jedaojeda/mva-hackathon-2026-pipeline](https://www.kaggle.com/code/jedaojeda/mva-hackathon-2026-pipeline)
- **Slug**: `jedaojeda/mva-hackathon-2026-pipeline`
- **Focus**: Consensus variant calling (GATK4 HaplotypeCaller + DeepVariant v1.6.0), AlphaMissense structural pathogenicity, Evo 2 7B DeltaLLR, and automated candidate ranking.

---

### 3. Track 2: De Novo Chemogenomics & Molecular Docking Engine
- **Direct Kaggle URL**: [https://www.kaggle.com/code/jedaojeda/mva-drug-repurposing-futurehouse](https://www.kaggle.com/code/jedaojeda/mva-drug-repurposing-futurehouse)
- **Slug**: `jedaojeda/mva-drug-repurposing-futurehouse`
- **Focus**: 3D conformational sampling (RDKit ETKDGv3), MMFF94 forcefield minimization, AutoDock Vina macromolecular docking, PROTAC ternary complex simulation, and Axolotl antimicrobial peptide evaluation.

---

### 4. Cloud Execution Outputs (`kaggle_cloud_outputs/`)
All direct computational outputs generated by the Kaggle GPU instances are synchronized inside this repository in both English and native execution forms:
- **`track1_wgs_deepscan_outputs/`**: 
  - `Consolidated_Clinical_Summary_Table_WGS_85GB.csv`: Clinical pathogenicity and allele frequencies across all lanes.
  - `Consolidated_NovaSeq_85GB_QC_Metrics.csv`: Multi-lane QC metrics (>94% Q30 across all flowcells).
  - `Master_Report_WGS_AlphaGenome_Exhaustive.csv`: Comprehensive chromatin accessibility and variant scoring profile.
  - `Dashboard_Clinico_WGS_AlphaGenome_85GB.html`: Interactive clinical visualizer.
  - `mva-wgs-alphagenome-deepscan.log`: Complete execution runtime logs.
- **`track1_pipeline_outputs/`**:
  - `submission_mva_track1_variants.csv`: Prioritized candidate variants from cloud pipeline.
  - `mva-hackathon-2026-pipeline.log`: Execution log with GATK4 and DeepVariant runtimes.
- **`track2_futurehouse_outputs/`**:
  - `docking_validation_results.csv` / `.json`: AutoDock Vina binding affinities and predicted Ki values for de novo candidates.
  - `candidate_drugs_prioritized.csv`: Multiaxial drug repurposing prioritization matrix.
  - `clinical_surveillance_protocol.csv`: Mechanistic oncologic and nephrologic surveillance protocol.
  - `De_Novo_20_Molecules_Library_MVA.csv` / `.sdf`: 3D conformations, physicochemical properties, and therapeutic axes.
  - `mva-drug-repurposing-futurehouse.log`: Complete chemogenomics runtime log.

*Local copies of all Jupyter notebooks (`.ipynb`) are included in this directory for offline execution.*
